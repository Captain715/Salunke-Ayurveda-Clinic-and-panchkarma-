07 / EVERYDAY LIVING

Make room for
daily rhythms.

Dinacharya is a Sanskrit term commonly used for daily routine in Ayurveda. Traditional discussions may include waking, personal care, meals, activity and rest.

01
NOTICE

Sleep & waking

Notice whether your sleep and wake times are reasonably consistent.

02
NOURISH

Regular meals

Make time for regular meals that suit your needs.

03
MOVE

Everyday activity

Include safe, age-appropriate movement in a way that fits your circumstances.

04
REST

Pause & recover

Allow time to rest and seek help when something feels wrong.

A useful routine should fit a person's age, responsibilities, health and circumstances. There is no need to follow a strict schedule from a website.

08 / SEASONAL LIVING

Living with
the seasons.

Understanding Ritucharya, a traditional concept describing seasonal routines in Ayurveda.

A TRADITIONAL CONCEPT

Life changes.
Our surroundings do too.

Ritucharya refers to seasonal regimen or seasonal routine. Classical Ayurvedic writing discusses how climate and seasonal transitions may influence everyday habits.

Practical choices should always consider local weather, personal comfort, medical needs and current public-health guidance.

KEEP IT SENSIBLE

Stay hydrated, dress for the weather, follow food-safety guidance and seek medical advice for health concerns. Traditional concepts should not replace medical care.
